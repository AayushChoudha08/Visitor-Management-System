# Role Permissions

## Employee Permissions
What they can see:
- Their own dashboard
- Their own invitations
- Their own visitors and assigned meetings
- Their own pre-approvals
- Relevant operational activity

What they can create:
- Visitor invitations
- Pre-approval passes
- Host-owned meeting records

What they can modify:
- Their own invitations and approval-related tasks when assigned to them
- Their own scheduled guest activity

What they cannot access:
- Full employee directory management
- All system logs or admin reports
- Front Desk security workflows
- System settings or policy admin functions

Why this role exists:
- The employee is the host and owns the invitation process. This role exists to manage visitor scheduling without exposing organization-wide administrative functions.

## Front Desk Permissions
What they can see:
- Today’s expected visitors
- Visitor directory and operational records
- Check-in and check-out queues
- Overstay status and arrival trends

What they can create:
- Visitor registration for walk-ins
- Manual check-in and check-out actions

What they can modify:
- Visit lifecycle states for physical access
- Visitor presence and departure records

What they cannot access:
- Employee management and role assignment
- Administrative configuration
- Invitation creation or host-level scheduling controls
- Full audit and settings surfaces intended for Admin

Why this role exists:
- Front Desk is responsible for physical entry/exit and verification. It exists to handle operational security rather than personnel or system administration.

## Admin Permissions
What they can see:
- All visitors
- All employees
- All invitations
- All visits
- System-wide activity logs
- Reports and configuration surfaces

What they can create:
- System-wide operational records when needed
- Administrative approvals and review actions

What they can modify:
- Settings, management records, and organization-level administrative controls
- Business approval and policy review flows

What they cannot access:
- Nothing critical outside the defined admin boundary

Why this role exists:
- Admin is the system-level role that manages organization-wide policies, reports, operational oversight, and the overall platform configuration.
